Business Glossary

LSOA

Lower Layer Super Output Area

Primary geographic unit used throughout the platform.

⸻

Housing Intelligence Index

Composite score measuring overall housing attractiveness.

Derived from:

* Affordability
* Crime
* Growth
* Investment

Range:

0–100

⸻

Area Rank

Relative ranking of all LSOAs.

Rank 1 = Highest Housing Intelligence Index.

⸻

Affordability Score

Relative affordability measure based on price and income relationships.

⸻

Growth Score

Relative housing market growth measure.

⸻

Investment Score

Relative investment attractiveness measure.
