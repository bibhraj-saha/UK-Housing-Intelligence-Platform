# Dashboard Gallery

This document showcases the dashboards available within the UK Housing Intelligence Platform. Each dashboard provides a different perspective on housing markets, investment opportunities, affordability, crime, income, accessibility, and regional performance across UK LSOAs.

---

## 1. Home

![Home](screenshots/Home.png)

### Description

The Home dashboard serves as the platform landing page and provides a high-level overview of the UK Housing Intelligence Platform.

**Key highlights:**

- Average UK house prices
- Average crime levels
- Investment score overview
- Housing intelligence index overview
- Platform capabilities and analytics coverage

## Platform Coverage

The UK Housing Intelligence Platform currently provides analytics across:

- 35,000+ UK LSOAs
- Housing Intelligence scoring
- Investment analytics
- Crime and affordability analysis
- Regional intelligence
- Income intelligence
- Historical market trends
- School accessibility
- Healthcare accessibility
- Transport accessibility
- Geographic intelligence mapping

The platform combines multiple national datasets into a unified analytics environment designed for property investors, researchers, analysts, and decision-makers.
